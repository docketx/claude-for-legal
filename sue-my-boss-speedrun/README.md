# sue-my-boss-speedrun

The fastest **honest** path from "this happened to me at work" to a claim that is still alive.

This plugin holds no legal rules of its own. Every legal statement comes from the `employment-law` pack in
this repository, where each one is a verbatim quote from a corpus we publish, re-checked against it, or is
recorded as not established. What this plugin adds is the **ordering** — which is the part people actually
get wrong.

## What "speedrun" means

**The preserving steps are urgent and almost all of them are free.** Write down what happened tonight. Keep
your own copy of what you already lawfully have. Understand that a clock may already be running and that it
probably started earlier than you think.

It does not mean the law is fast, that suing is a good idea, or that there is a shortcut past the parts that
need a lawyer. Nothing here will tell you that you have a claim.

## The order

1. **Preserve, tonight.** Your own dated account in your own words. Your contract, pay records, reviews,
   messages you were a party to, the names of anyone who saw it. This costs nothing and is the thing people
   most regret not having.
2. **Understand the clock.** A short one probably applies, and it may have started when you were *told*, not
   when the job ended.
3. **Know what gets filed before a lawsuit.** For discrimination there is normally an agency charge first,
   and the deadline in step 2 is the deadline for *that*, not for a court case. This is the step most people
   have never heard of.
4. **Get a lawyer onto it** — with the record already assembled, which is the part you can do yourself.

## Preserve without making it worse

Collect what you **already lawfully have**. Do **not** take confidential documents you are not entitled to,
break a system rule to get evidence, or record someone where that is unlawful. Any of those can be a
fireable act in itself and can damage the very claim you are preserving.

## Skills

| skill | for |
|---|---|
| `sue-my-boss-speedrun` | "something happened, what do I do now" — the ordering above |
| `did-they-break-fmla` | leave denied, leave counted against you, discipline for taking leave |
| `whats-my-deadline` | "how long do I have", "is it too late", "should I wait until my last day" |

## The three things this plugin will never do

1. **Never tell you whether you have a claim.** Your facts are not in the pack.
2. **Never compute a deadline.** The deadline rules it can verify come from 1980 opinions. They establish
   that a short clock exists and that it starts earlier than people expect. They are a reason to call a
   lawyer today, not a date you can rely on. It will not tell you that you still have time, and it will not
   tell you that you are out of time either.
3. **Never name or characterise a real employer, manager or colleague.**

## Coverage

Federal, California, Texas, Washington and New Jersey today. Every other state is reported as not covered rather than answered
from general knowledge. The preserving steps are not jurisdiction-specific and are given regardless.

Federal statutory phrases are quoted from the United States Code, which we publish. Court holdings are quoted
from the opinions. See the `employment-law` README for what is still not covered.

Every output is a draft for attorney review, not legal advice.
